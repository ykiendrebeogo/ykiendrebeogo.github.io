Youssouf Kiendrebeogo

I am an Economist at the International Monetary Fund with over a decade of experience in research and operational work. My research interests include financing for sustainable development, macro-fiscal surveillance, and country engagement and program monitoring.

Previously, I held positions at the World Bank, notably in the Chief Economist Office for the Middle East & North Africa region and the Macro, Trade & Investment Global Practice.

My work has been published in peer-reviewed journals including Structural Change & Economic Dynamics, World Economy, Journal of Development Studies, and Review of Development Economics.

I received my Ph.D. in Economics in 2014 from the University of Auvergne (CERDI), where I also taught econometrics.
