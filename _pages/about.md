I am a Senior Economist at the [International Monetary Fund](https://www.imf.org) with over a decade of experience in research and operational work. My research interests include financing for sustainable development, macro-fiscal surveillance, and country engagement and program monitoring.

Previously, I held positions at the [World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org), notably in the Chief Economist Office for the Middle East & North Africa region and the Macro, Trade & Investment Global Practice.

My work has been published in peer-reviewed journals including [Structural Change & Economic Dynamics](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/structural-change-and-economic-dynamics), [World Economy](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14679701), [Journal of Development Studies](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/fjds20), and [Review of Development Economics](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/14679361).

I received my Ph.D. in Economics in 2014 from the [University of Auvergne (CERDI)](https://cerdi.uca.fr/), where I also taught econometrics.
